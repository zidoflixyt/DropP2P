📖 About DropP2P, Features & FAQ Guide

What is DropP2P?

DropP2P is a free, serverless peer-to-peer app that lets two devices connect directly over encrypted WebRTC to share files of any size, chat in real time, exchange voice notes and use a push-to-talk walkie-talkie. No accounts, no cloud storage, no tracking.

Key Features

• Direct P2P file sharing up to 5 GB+ with memory-safe streaming
• Encrypted real-time chat and voice notes
• Push-to-talk Walkie-Talkie with live audio
• QR code or 6-digit code pairing, works locally and remotely
• Consent gate: nothing is downloaded without your approval
• Ghost Mode and local blocking for full control

FAQ

Is it really serverless? Yes — content travels only between the two paired devices.
How do I pair? Show a QR code or share your 6-digit room code; the other device scans or types it.
How big can files be? Multi-gigabyte files stream in 64 KB chunks straight to disk where supported.
Is it free? Yes. DropCoins and passes are optional and support development.
